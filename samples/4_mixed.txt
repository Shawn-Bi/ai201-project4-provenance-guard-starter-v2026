When working on an AI phone-ordering agent at ZBS POS, the hardest part was not getting the AI to answer questions based on the menu. It was getting the agent to complete orders reliably despite noisy phone calls, peak-hour concurrency, and frequently changing menus. More challenging still, customers asked questions outside the menu and knowledge base, where giving a wrong answer could be more dangerous than not answering. I was responsible for translating frontline feedback into agent workflows and prompts, and demonstrating measurable improvement. This mattered because calls and dine-in orders peak at the same time. One incorrect order means wasted food preparation and refunds. And if the model confidently invents an answer to a question outside the knowledge base, the restaurant bears the consequences. So accuracy, stability, and knowing what to do when the answer is unknown all had to be addressed together.
